Tomato Rhythm · A Pomodoro and focus timer for iPhone and iPad

Get support

Email ride-wind@foxmail.com. Include your app and iOS versions, device model, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

Getting started

Choose a saved configuration or set focus and rest durations. Free runs one cycle. Plus and Pro support multiple or unlimited cycles and optional long rests. By default, confirm each finished countdown before the next stage. Settings also offers automatic advancement with a 0–30 second delay.

Plus and Pro

Plus includes cycles, long rests, personalization, Liquid Glass, scheduled configurations, and cross-device sync. Pro includes all Plus benefits and adds chained plans, groups, scheduled plans, complete history, statistics, exports, and guardian mode. See the in-app benefits page for current availability.

Restore purchases

Choose Restore Purchases on the membership page using the original App Store purchasing account. The purchasing and iCloud accounts can differ. Plus and Pro are one-time purchases, not subscriptions. Apple handles refunds.

On-demand networking

Ordinary startup uses local data. Open the membership page to load Apple products or verify and restore purchases. For sync, explicitly choose Connect iCloud on the sync page. This permits later automatic sync; stopping the connection keeps your data.

Sync

No personal server is needed. Optional sync uses your private Apple iCloud database. Sign in to the same iCloud account on your devices, allow iCloud for the app, and ensure your membership supports sync. Background scheduling and network conditions affect timing.

Guardian mode

A guardian must verify their chosen password to exit. Ordinary editing is restricted while the mode is active; appearance settings remain adjustable. Protected records and their configuration snapshots remain protected after exit and cannot be edited or deleted within the app.

Forgotten guardian passwords

Prepare another updated iPhone or iPad. Open Settings → Guardian mode → Guardian Verification, authenticate with the system, then scan the temporary QR created on the protected device. To confirm the first save, enter the four requested character positions on the protected device. Later password changes or deletion require the full recovery code. New codes have six case-sensitive alphanumeric characters. QR codes expire after 90 seconds and cannot be imported on the device that created them. Scanning, saving and entering codes work offline; the verification device does not need a purchase. Keep QR images and recovery codes private. The developer cannot remotely recover a password without a previously saved recovery code.

iPad and landscape

Layouts adapt to actual window dimensions. iPhone supports landscape; iPad supports rotation and resized windows. Layout changes preserve the current timer and paused state. Windowed low-light views use an overlay; system brightness is adjusted only in a foreground full-screen window.

Reminders and system displays

Notifications, sound, vibration, photos, and Dynamic Island depend on permissions, device capabilities, and system behavior. Manage these options in app and system settings.

Privacy

Core timing works offline; optional iCloud sync and purchases use Apple services. Read our Privacy Policy.
