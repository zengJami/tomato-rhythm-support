Tomato Rhythm · A Pomodoro and focus timer for iPhone

Get support

Email ride-wind@foxmail.com. Include your app and iOS versions, device model, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

Getting started

Choose a saved configuration or set focus and rest durations. Free runs one cycle. Plus and Pro support multiple or unlimited cycles and optional long rests. Confirm each finished countdown before the next stage.

Plus and Pro

Plus includes cycles, long rests, personalization, Liquid Glass, scheduled configurations, and cross-device sync. Pro includes all Plus benefits and adds chained plans, groups, scheduled plans, complete history, statistics, exports, and guardian mode. See the in-app benefits page for current availability.

Restore purchases

Choose Restore Purchases on the membership page using the original App Store purchasing account. The purchasing and iCloud accounts can differ. Plus and Pro are one-time purchases, not subscriptions. Apple handles refunds.

Sync

No personal server is needed. Optional sync uses your private Apple iCloud database. Sign in to the same iCloud account on your devices, allow iCloud for the app, and ensure your membership supports sync. Background scheduling and network conditions affect timing.

Guardian mode

A guardian must verify their chosen password to exit. Protected records and configurations cannot be modified or deleted while the mode is active; appearance settings remain adjustable.

Reminders and system displays

Notifications, sound, vibration, photos, and Dynamic Island depend on permissions, device capabilities, and system behavior. Manage these options in app and system settings.

Privacy

Core timing works offline; optional iCloud sync and purchases use Apple services. Read our Privacy Policy.
