Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Core timer features work offline. Optional cross-device sync transmits and stores relevant data in your private Apple iCloud database. Purchases are processed by Apple.

We do not operate our own user-data servers, include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs in the app, or sell your personal data.

Scope

This policy applies to Tomato Rhythm (番茄节律), an iPhone and iPad app provided by zeng xiongbing (曾雄兵). You do not need a separate developer-operated app account. Apple provides Apple Account, iCloud, and App Store services.

Data on your device

The app stores your configurations, plans, groups, schedules, appearance and reminder settings, focus history, pause events, and photos you choose. Guardian verification information is kept in device security storage. This data supports saved preferences, timing, history, and protected guardian-mode records.

The app does not access your entire photo library without your selection, or request advertising tracking, contacts, or location permissions.

Optional iCloud sync

When you enable an eligible cross-device sync feature and sign in to iCloud, the app uses Apple CloudKit to transmit configurations, preferences, plans, records, selected photos, device identifiers, and session state needed for sync and session handoff to your Apple Account's private database.

Apple iCloud stores this data under your Apple Account and Apple's terms. Turning sync off does not automatically delete data already stored in iCloud. Whether system backups include local app data depends on your Apple backup settings.

Purchases

Plus and Pro use one-time Apple In-App Purchases through StoreKit. Apple handles payment and refunds; we do not receive payment card information. The app reads and verifies product information and valid purchase entitlements returned by Apple to unlock features and restore purchases.

Photos and system displays

You may choose photos for backgrounds and timer displays. If enabled, selected photos and timer state may appear in notifications, on the Lock Screen, or in Dynamic Island. You control these options in the app and system notification settings.

Managing and deleting data

You can edit and delete supported data in the app. While guardian mode is active, a guardian must verify their password to exit before ordinary editing resumes. Records created under protection and their configuration snapshots remain protected after exit and cannot be edited or deleted within the app. The app also refuses its bulk cloud-deletion operation when protected records are present. With sync enabled, deletion changes propagate according to sync status.

Uninstalling removes local app data but may leave verification information in Keychain, data in iCloud, and Apple purchase records. You can also use Apple's iCloud storage management features to manage the app's cloud data. Manage purchase records and refund requests through Apple.

General audience and guardians

The app is for a general audience and provides optional guardian mode. It has no public chat or social community and does not use advertising profiles. Guardians should help minors choose appropriate device, photo, notification, and iCloud settings.

Device authentication, scanning and offline recovery codes

A guardian may optionally scan a temporary QR code with another device and save an independent recovery code to change or delete a forgotten guardian password. Verification is free on the guardian device. The QR carries a temporary bearer credential from which a recovery code can be derived; it does not contain the plain guardian password. Anyone holding the QR can obtain the recovery code, so show it only to a trusted guardian device. New QR codes expire after 90 seconds. An app refuses to import its own QR using a local random Keychain identifier; this is not hardware identity verification. New recovery codes have six case-sensitive alphanumeric characters. Their protection relies on local security and attempt limits; keep them private.

The camera is used only when you choose to scan. You may instead select a QR image from Photos for on-device recognition. Scanning, saving and entering codes do not transmit them through a developer server, local network or iCloud. The guardian device stores saved recovery codes and descriptions in Keychain; the protected device stores a salted digest and local setup credentials. Recovery codes do not participate in app sync. Keychain items may remain after uninstalling; manage and delete saved codes in Guardian Verification.

Entering Guardian Verification and deleting a saved code use Apple device-owner authentication, which may use Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode. The app receives the authentication result, not biometric templates, face images, fingerprints or your system passcode. Leaving the page or backgrounding the app locks that page again.

On-demand networking and offline purchase access

Ordinary local timer startup does not initiate purchase or cloud-account verification. Opening the purchase page or restoring purchases uses Apple services. Explicitly connecting iCloud authorizes later automatic networking for that feature. Stopping the connection revokes automatic sync consent and keeps existing local and cloud data. Apple-verified non-consumable product identifiers may be cached in local Keychain for offline access in recognized distribution environments. This cache grants no new purchases, is not synced between devices and reflects refunds or account changes after the next online verification.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to attach, and use it to respond to your request. Do not send Apple Account passwords, verification codes, payment card details, or unnecessary private records.

This website and service providers

This static support site is hosted on GitHub Pages and contains no developer-operated analytics scripts or forms. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. For Apple services, see Apple's Privacy Policy.
